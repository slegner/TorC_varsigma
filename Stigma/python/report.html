Stigma Q-torsion vs DESI DR2

JAX/diffrax pipeline + BlackJAX Nested Slice Sampling — progress report
PhD research · University of Cambridge · May 2025

Overview

The Stigma Q-torsion theory is a modified gravity model in which a scalar field ϖ (varpi) couples non-minimally to geometry. The goal of this pipeline is to confront the theory against the DESI DR2 BAO dataset (13 distance measurements) using Bayesian nested sampling, producing posteriors on the Stigma parameters ς, −υ₂, ϖ₀ as well as the standard cosmological parameters Ωm, ΩΛ, H₀rd.

The original numerical solver (equations_double.py, scipy + numpy) has been fully converted to JAX + diffrax, and the DESI likelihood wrapped for use with BlackJAX Nested Slice Sampling (NSS). The pipeline is JIT-compiled and runs at 4.8 ms per likelihood evaluation (cached), enabling practical nested sampling on a laptop CPU.

Three-stage ODE solve

  • 1
    Integrate from τini with placeholder a₀=1 until da/dτ = H₀norm. The scale factor at that event gives the normalisation constant a₀.
  • 2a
    Re-integrate with correct a₀ until a = 1. Reads τtoday and H₀actual = da/dτ|a=1 directly from the ODE, guaranteeing E(z=0) = 1 exactly.
  • 2b
    Solve again from τini to τtoday on a log-spaced grid of 500 points (for NSS) / 2000 points (for validation). Returns the a(τ) and aH(τ) arrays used to compute distances.

Derived quantity

The sound horizon is derived internally from the sampled H₀ rd and the H₀actual read from the ODE. This keeps the distance computation self-consistent without fixing rd to a Planck value.

Key Physics Notes

Why OmL is sampled

Stigma is a modification of gravity, not simply dark energy added to ΛCDM. The Stigma field can provide an accelerated expansion without a cosmological constant. However, a mixed model (Stigma + Λ) is also physically valid. By sampling ΩΛ as a free parameter, the posterior will naturally reveal whether the DESI data prefer pure Stigma (ΩΛ→0), ΛCDM-like (ΩΛ∼0.7), or something in between.

Why h0rd is sampled instead of H0 and rd separately

DESI measures distance ratios D/rd. The sound horizon rd depends on pre-recombination physics (primarily ωb and Neff), which are not part of the Stigma ODE. Sampling the product H₀ rd marginalises over this degeneracy, and the sound horizon is derived as rd = (H₀ rd) / H₀actual using the H₀ read from the ODE at a=1.

Stigma viability constraints

The theory requires two conditions to be well-posed (no ghost, no gradient instability):

c₁ = ς − ϖ₀² > 0    and    c₂ = ϖ₀² − ς(5 + 4ς(−2 + ϖ₀²)) > 0

These are enforced as hard priors in logprior_fn.

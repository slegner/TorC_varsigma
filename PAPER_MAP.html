TorC against DESI DR2

Everything established so far, sorted into the sections of a paper — with the numbers, the figure that carries each claim, and what is still missing.

Data DESI DR2 BAO, 13 measurements Sampling 1000 live, nested slice Runs 5 production chains Tests 8/8 passing

DESI DR2 BAO cannot distinguish TorC from ΛCDM. Across four defensible priors on ς the Bayes factor runs from 1.24 : 1 for TorC to 2.07 : 1 against, passing through exact parity — while the mean likelihood stays flat to 0.04 nats.

The fit never changes; only the Occam penalty does. That, not any single Bayes factor, is the result the paper should lead with.

§1

Theory: an exact scaling symmetry

strongest new material

Every term of the TorC background Friedmann equation is homogeneous of degree two in time derivatives — H², H₀²S(a)/υ₂, 2Hϖϖ̇, ϖ̇², and the squared bracket all carry exactly two rates. This yields a one-parameter symmetry:

Ωr,0, Ωm,0, ΩΛ,0 → κ²ΩH(a) → κH(a)ϖ̇ → κϖ̇ϖ(a) unchangedτ → τ/κ

Every term acquires exactly κ², so the equation is invariant, and E(z) = H/H(a=1) divides the factor out. Three separate facts collapse into this one symmetry:

  • υ₂ is a gauge — a choice of time units. Writing τ = √υ₂ σ removes it from the equations of motion and the series initial conditions. verified 1e-9
  • MP is a choice of mass units — absorbed into the definition Ωi = ρi/(3MP²H₀²); it does not appear in the equations at all.
  • Only ratios of the Ω's are measurable — their overall scale is the same direction as υ₂.

ς survives as the one physical parameter because it appears in three independent places in the Lagrangian, so no single renaming removes it. υ₂ appears once.

Method: solver and sampler

methods section

Integration in x = ln a from the early-time series to a fixed endpoint x = 0, so each evaluation is a single ODE solve on a static grid — no event, no calibration pass. Today is defined as a = 1 and E is normalised there, so no H₀ enters anywhere.

Two method points worth a paragraph each:

  • Out-of-bounds proposals. The slice sampler proposes outside the prior; an out-of-support solve burns the full step budget and, because vmapped lanes run in lockstep, stalls the whole batch. Clamping to a safe fiducial before the solve and flooring logL afterwards gives identical results at a fraction of the cost.
  • Solver choice was benchmarked properly. A work–precision diagram (runtime vs achieved error), not runtime at a requested tolerance — those differ by orders of magnitude between solvers. Implicit solvers only look catastrophic when their root-finder inherits tolerances from the stepsize controller; set explicitly they cost ~4–10×, not 500×.

The (Ωm, ΩΛ) wedge

The posterior is a wedge pointing at the origin because the likelihood is. Scaling both Ω's together moves χ² by 0.79 across a factor of 7.5 in Ωm; changing ΩΛ alone by a factor of two moves it by 230. The degeneracy is broken only by Ωra⁻⁴, which is under 0.1% of the source over z < 2.6 — so the wedge runs until the prior stops it.

Results: model comparison is prior-dominated

the headline

ΛCDM: ln Z = −7.734 ± 0.094, DKL = 8.26. Four TorC runs, identical in every respect except the prior on ς:

Three things to make of this:

  • All four are |Δ ln Z| < 1 — inconclusive on the Jeffreys scale. The sign changes with the prior.
  • ⟨ln L⟩ is flat to 0.04 nats. Since ln Z = ⟨ln L⟩ − DKL, the entire 0.94-nat spread is the Occam term. Between λ = 0.5 and λ = 2, Δln Z = −0.94 and ΔDKL = +0.95 — they agree to 0.01. Widening the prior did not make TorC fit worse; it made the posterior harder to locate.
  • Width is not the whole story. The uniform prior sits below both exponentials that bracket it in width. What costs evidence is prior–posterior mismatch, not prior width.

The posterior on ς is robust (1.28–1.42 across a 4× change in prior width); the evidence is not. Quote ς with confidence; do not quote a single Bayes factor.

Before this goes out

honest gaps
  • claimυ₂ becoming physical at perturbation level is unverified. Both priors.py and scaling_symmetry.tex §4 state it as established. A comoving k does not obviously break the degeneracy — the sound horizon and DM both pick up √υ₂, so DM/rd stays invariant. Either derive it or label it a conjecture.
  • transcriptionCheck §1 of the .tex against your source. I transcribed the Lagrangian and Friedmann equation from screenshots. Everything downstream follows from rate-counting, so a slip would not invalidate the argument — but it would be wrong in print.
  • statsOne seed per prior. Run-to-run scatter is not measured at 1000 live. Two or three seeds on one configuration would let you quote it.
  • statsnum_delete = 100 (10% of live) is unvalidated for ln Z bias. Flagged earlier and never resolved.
  • priorΩΛ hits its prior ceiling. 7.3% of posterior mass within 0.05 of 1.5, so that boundary contributes its own Occam penalty to every TorC ln Z. Worth one run with a wider ΩΛ prior to separate it from the ς effect.
  • scopeBAO only. No SNe, no CMB. The ς constraint is 19% from BAO alone; adding datasets is the obvious way to make the comparison conclusive rather than prior-dominated.
  • defnSettle what "matter fraction" means in TorC before printing 0.2216. The denominator excludes the ϖ contribution, and the Friedmann equation carries a (ϖ²−4ς) prefactor.
